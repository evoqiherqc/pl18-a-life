PL18 — a life

A 19th-century Burmese palm-leaf manuscript, in Glasgow since 1873. No one at the University currently reads it.

This site is a portfolio: a wordless film, a 3D model of the manuscript, the catalogue record, and a paradata legend that colour-codes every claim by its source.

PL18 is a Burmese palm-leaf manuscript holding the verses of the Dhammapada — gifted by a Burmese Buddhist monk to a school in Edinburgh in the 1830s, and acquired by the University of Glasgow in 1873.

The catalogue is brief, careful, and partly speculative. The film below is wordless, told from the manuscript's point of view — alongside a careful record of where the AI helped, where it had to be corrected, and where it was simply wrong.

The film below was made entirely with image and video AI tools (Nano Banana for stills, Veo 3.1 for animation). It follows PL18 from creation in Burma to its arrival in Glasgow and into the present-day digitisation work. The transitions between clips are AI infrastructure showing through, not editorial choices — see the methodology notebook for why.
